# Student Leave Regulations and Protocols

## Casual Leave and Absence Notification
Students are eligible for up to six days of approved casual leave per academic semester for personal, domestic, or emergent circumstances. A leave application must be submitted via the student portal at least 24 hours prior to the intended leave date, accompanied by parental or guardian authorization.

## Medical Leave and Health Center Certification
In the event of severe illness or hospitalization, medical leave up to a maximum of 15 calendar days per semester may be recognized. The student or guardian must notify the Department Head within 48 hours of illness onset. Upon return, the student must report to the University Health Center for fitness verification with original hospital discharge summaries and medical prescriptions.

## Duty Leave for Competitions and Conferences
Students representing the university in state or national sports events, technical hackathons, academic conferences, or cultural symposiums are granted On-Duty (OD) attendance credit. The faculty coordinator of the respective club or sports director must endorse and submit the OD approval form to the Academic Registrar prior to the event.

## Long-Term Semester Withdrawal
A student who experiences prolonged medical incapacitation requiring absence of more than four continuous weeks may petition the Academic Council for a formal Semester Withdrawal without academic penalty. Courses for that semester will be marked as 'Withdrawn' (W) and tuition fee adjustment is governed by university refund bylaws.
