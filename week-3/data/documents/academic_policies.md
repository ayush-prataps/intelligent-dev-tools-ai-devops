# University Academic Policies and Degree Requirements

## Degree Credit Structure and Course Registration
Undergraduate engineering degree programs require the successful completion of a minimum of 160 academic credits distributed across Basic Sciences, Engineering Core, Professional Electives, Open Electives, and Capstone Projects. Course registration occurs during the first three days of each semester through the Student Information System. Late registration is permitted up to five days with an approved penalty fee.

## Course Prerequisite Policy
Students cannot register for an advanced course without successfully clearing its listed prerequisite subjects with a passing grade. Any conditional prerequisite waiver requires written recommendation from the course instructor and approval by the Departmental Academic Committee prior to course registration.

## Academic Probation and CGPA Retention
A student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 5.0 at the end of each academic year to remain in good academic standing. If a student's CGPA falls below 5.0, they will be placed on Academic Probation for one semester. Students on probation have their maximum registered credits capped at 18 credits and must attend mandatory remedial advisory sessions.

## Honors and Minor Degrees
Students with a consistent CGPA of 8.0 or higher, with no historical course backlogs, may opt for an Honors degree in their primary discipline or a Minor degree in a secondary engineering or computing specialization by completing an additional 18–20 specialized credits between semesters 4 and 8.
