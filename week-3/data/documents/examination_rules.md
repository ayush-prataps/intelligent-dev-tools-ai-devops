# University Examination Rules and Regulations

## Examination Hall Entry and Admit Card
Students must arrive at the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled commencement of the examination. No candidate will be admitted after 30 minutes from the start time. Every student must present a valid physical University Identity Card and official Examination Admit Card signed by the Controller of Examinations. Digital ID cards on mobile devices are not permitted.

## Prohibited Items and Electronics
Mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, Bluetooth earphones, storage media, and unauthorized written materials are strictly prohibited inside the examination venue. Possession of any electronic communication device, whether powered on or off, is categorized as a Category-1 examination violation and results in immediate confiscation and disciplinary investigation.

## Grading System and Passing Standards
The university operates on a 10-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system. Course grades range from 'O' (Outstanding - 10 points) down to 'P' (Pass - 4 points) and 'F' (Fail - 0 points). To successfully earn course credits, a student must secure at least 40% marks in the end-semester examination and an aggregate of 45% when combined with continuous internal evaluations.

## Supplementary Examinations and Revaluation
Students who receive an 'F' grade in a theoretical course may appear for a Supplementary Examination conducted before the start of the following academic year. Students dissatisfied with their evaluation may submit a formal request for answer script revaluation or transparency photocopy within ten working days of result declaration through the Controller of Examinations portal.
