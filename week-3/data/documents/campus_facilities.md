# University Campus Facilities and Resource Access

## Central Library and Digital Resource Timings
The Central University Library remains open Monday through Saturday from 08:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and on Sundays and recognized holidays from 10:00 AM to 06:00 PM. During final examination weeks, reading halls operate on a 24-hour schedule. Registered students may borrow up to four books simultaneously for a borrowing period of 14 calendar days using their barcode-enabled Student ID Card.

## High-Performance Computing and Cloud Labs
The Computing Center provides access to high-performance GPU clusters, cloud development sandboxes, and virtualized container environments for authorized coursework and faculty-mentored research. Access is granted through university institutional Single Sign-On (SSO). Accounts inactive for 90 days are automatically decommissioned.

## Maker Space and Hardware Prototyping
The Advanced Innovation Maker Space offers 3D printers, laser cutters, CNC milling units, and electronics test benches. All students must complete mandatory Workshop Safety Orientation prior to operating heavy machinery. Consumables such as PLA filament and soldering supplies are requisitioned through the department portal.

## Sports Complex and Fitness Facilities
The indoor sports arena encompasses badminton courts, table tennis stations, squash courts, and a supervised gymnasium. Facility timings are 06:00 AM to 09:00 AM in the morning and 04:30 PM to 08:30 PM in the evening. Clean non-marking sports shoes and valid sports registration tags are mandatory for arena entry.
