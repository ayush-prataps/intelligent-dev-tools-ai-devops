# University Attendance Policy

## Minimum Attendance Requirement
All registered undergraduate and postgraduate students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each enrolled theory and laboratory course during a semester to be eligible for end-semester examinations. Attendance is calculated from the official semester commencement date until the last instructional day announced in the academic calendar.

## Condonation of Attendance Shortage
Students with attendance between 65% and 74.9% may apply to the Dean of Academic Affairs for attendance condonation on genuine medical or compassionate grounds. The application must include an official medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner within seven working days of returning to campus, along with a nominal condonation fee. Condonation is granted at the sole discretion of the Academic Review Board.

## Debarment Due to Attendance Shortage
Students with aggregate course attendance below 65% under any circumstances are considered debarred from appearing in the end-semester examination for that specific course. Debarred students receive an 'F-Attendance' (FA) grade and are required to re-register for the course in a subsequent semester or summer term when the course is offered.

## Attendance Monitoring and Discrepancies
Course instructors update daily attendance in the University Academic ERP portal within 48 hours of conducting each class. Students are responsible for reviewing their attendance record weekly. Any discrepancy must be reported in writing to the respective course coordinator within five business days of attendance posting.
